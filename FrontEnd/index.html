Frequently Asked Questions

What if I do not have a smartphone?

You can use any basic mobile phone to text: BOOK WHEAT 50 9876543210 or check status with STATUS #KRN-1234.

How are MSP prices updated?

Prices are synchronized daily according to official Central Government Minimum Support Price guidelines.
